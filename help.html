FAQs

Good questions

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp or call +27 64 762 9443.

Do you deliver?

Ask us. Send your order and address on WhatsApp and we'll tell you what's possible and what it costs before you pay.

What is 7Waves?

7Waves is our delivery partner for fast local orders. Pick "7Waves" in your bag, copy your list and order the same items on 7Waves.

Can I order online and collect in the shop?

Yes. Choose "Collect" in your bag. We'll let you know on WhatsApp when it's packed and waiting for you at Lambton.

How do I pay?

Pay in the shop when you collect, or order on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the total and how to pay before anything is sent.

Is everything on the website really in stock?

The website only lists products that are in stock on our stock list. Things do sell fast, so if something goes before we confirm your order, we'll tell you straight away and suggest something similar.

Do I need to be 18?

Yes. We only sell to adults and check ID in the shop, on collection and on delivery.

Which coil or pod fits my device?

Use our Coil & Pod Finder: pick your device and it shows every coil, pod and tank that fits, in stock now. Still not sure? Send us a photo of your device on WhatsApp.

My device stopped working. What now?

Message us with your order details and what's happening. Warranty depends on the brand, but we'll help you sort it out.

Do you only sell vapes?

No. We also stock Dubai-inspired perfumes and tech & gadgets. Ask us on WhatsApp what's in, or come and have a look.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes, we sell wholesale and retail. Call or WhatsApp 064 762 9443 with what you need.

Can you get a product you don't have?

Often, yes. Tell us what you're after and we'll check with our suppliers.
